## Before the event

Register, and you will get a confirmation email straight away. Joining links arrive by email **three days before** the conference, with a short guide to the platforms.

You need a recent web browser and a stable internet connection. A headset helps in workshops and networking rooms.

## During the sessions

- **Talks and keynotes** stream live. Ask questions in the chat and the session chair will put them to the speaker.
- **Workshops** run in smaller rooms with cameras and microphones on. Places are limited, so sign up in advance.
- **The poster hall** stays open for all three days. Presenters are live at their posters during the poster sessions in the programme.

## Time zones

All times on this site are in the conference time zone. The [programme](../programme/) can switch to your own time zone.

## Accessibility

Live captions are on for every streamed session. Slides are shared in advance where speakers agree. If you need an adjustment, contact the organisers when you register and we will do our best to make it work.

## Recordings

Keynotes and contributed talks are recorded when the speaker agrees, and shared with registered attendees for 30 days. Workshops, networking rooms and the poster hall are not recorded.
