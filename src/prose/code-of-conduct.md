## Our commitment

Everyone who takes part in VIBE, including attendees, speakers, organisers, volunteers and sponsors, agrees to help keep the conference welcoming, respectful and free from harassment.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of other people, their work and their time.
- Criticise ideas, not people. Keep questions to the point so others can ask too.
- Use the chat and poster hall for the conference, not for promotion or recruitment unrelated to the session.
- Ask before recording, screenshotting or sharing someone else's slides, posters or words outside the conference.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment of any kind is not acceptable. This includes offensive comments about gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, appearance, age, ethnicity, nationality, religion or career stage; unwelcome sexual attention; intimidation; sustained disruption of sessions; and publishing someone's private information.

## Reporting

If something happens that makes you or someone else uncomfortable, tell a member of the organising committee in a private chat, or email the organisers. Reports are handled in confidence. You will not be asked to confront anyone.

## What happens next

The organisers may warn the person, remove them from a session or from the conference, and contact their institution where appropriate. We will follow up with the person who reported.
