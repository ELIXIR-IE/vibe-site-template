The Virtual Institute of Bioinformatics brings together researchers, students, research software engineers and data stewards for three days of talks, posters and hands-on workshops. It is online, so anyone can join from anywhere, and it is free, so cost is never the reason someone stays away.

## What to expect

- **Keynotes** from people leading their fields, with time for real questions.
- **Contributed and lightning talks** selected from open abstracts, with a strong place for early-career researchers.
- **Workshops** where you leave with something working on your own laptop.
- **A poster hall** with live sessions, so posters get conversations as well as views.

## Who organises it

VIBE moves around the community: each year a different institution hosts it. A volunteer organising committee and programme committee, drawn from across the partner institutions, run each edition. See the [committees](../committees/).

## The logo

The mark is the whole island drawn as a sequencing flow cell. Each coral well is a campus of a partner institution, so the logo is also a map of the community.
